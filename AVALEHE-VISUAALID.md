# Avalehe pildi ja logode töötlus

Avalehe illustratsioon ning Rocca Sörgi logo väljalõige valmisid sisseehitatud imagegeni tööriistaga. Algseid kasutaja faile ei muudetud.

## Failid projektis

- `public/assets/tenerife-sunrise-runner.png`: avalehe uus illustratiivne pilt. See ei kinnita ühegi konkreetse jooksuraja asukohta ega läbitavust.
- `public/assets/uploads/rocca-sork-logo-black.png`: fotolt eraldatud must Rocca Sörgi sõnalogo, läbipaistev taust.
- `public/assets/uploads/on-logo-black.webp`: kasutaja saadetud ON-i logo muutmata koopia, olemasoleva läbipaistva taustaga.

Pilti saab muuta halduses „Pildid / Images” ning logode nähtavust ja järjestust jaotises „Logo & sponsors / Logo ja toetajad”.

Toetajate logosid kuvatakse jaluses ligikaudu sõnalogo kõrguselt. SVG värvifilter annab mõlema logo olemasolevale läbipaistvale kujule Runographi heleda jalusekirjaga sama värvi (#f4f5eb). Algseid logofaile ei muudeta.

## Avalehe pildi loomise juhis

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide running travel blog homepage hero photograph. Create a highly realistic editorial sports travel photograph of one anonymous adult runner seen entirely from behind, running away from the camera on a quiet asphalt mountain road in Tenerife at sunrise. The road winds through rugged volcanic slopes and dry ochre mountain terrain, with a soft sea of clouds lower down in the distance, warm low sunrise light touching the landscape from the upper right, muted blue shadows, natural subtle film grain. Runner wears simple charcoal running shorts, a muted olive technical T-shirt and neutral running shoes, no logos. Camera behind runner at chest height, medium-wide landscape composition, runner on the right-hand third with full body visible, about one quarter of frame height, a believable candid running stride. Road and landscape span the full image; keep the left half visually calm and darker with uncluttered volcanic mountain silhouettes for readable cream website headline text. Landscape is the hero, human small enough to provide scale. Asphalt must look real with natural surface detail, no cars, no visible road signs, no text, no watermark, no athletic brand marks. Wide horizontal 3:2 composition, photographic realism, restrained warm sunrise colors, no oversaturation, no fantasy geology. This is an illustrative Tenerife scene, not a claim to depict a verified route.
